### Introduction

As AI advances, the line between human and machine becomes blurred, making it harder for people to distinguish between real and artificial. VeriSocial addresses this issue by providing a platform for users and implementing a system that detects and filters out AI-generated content or Bot activity, ensuring that users can experience an authentic online experience.

### Background

> The idea for VeriSocial stems from the growing concern around the rise of AI generated content and bot activity online. Overtime AI has advanced to a point where it becomes increasingly difficult for users to distinguish between authentic human interaction and automated or artificially generated posts. We see this especially in many social media platforms today such X (Twitter). This overall dilutes the online experience for users as browsing becomes more crowded with non-human activity. For our app VeriSocial, we aim to create a space that filters out AI content, allowing users to engage with others confidently knowing that they are interacting with real people. No Bots or AIs.

### Achievements

> The idea behind the project is to try to mitigate the rise of AI-generated content in social media. We aim to create a service that people can use to both “verify” posts, and thus our users can enjoy authentic content with a certain degree of guarantee that what they are seeing was made by a person.

> The project must achieve a stable status as a social media. This means creating a system that can be monitored, be reliable and secure. 

> Observability must be a priority for our project, as this is how we will be able to monitor how well people are reacting to our different detection methods inside our product. 


### Justification

> For users who value authentic human interaction and content, VeriSocial will provide a platform free from AI content and Bots. Many of today’s social platforms are plagued with AI generated content and Bot activity (examples include AI Art, Spam replies and fake spam accounts). Now more than ever, there is an increasing reliance on AI, making the demand for authentic content more valuable.
